The InstaDoc Story

The Problem: For too long, clinical teams have wrestled with fragmented spreadsheets, manual forms and the chaos of old-school metric tracking — producing noise, not insight.

The Solution: InstaDoc was born to bridge the gap between complex medical data and simple, actionable insight. We distilled clinician workflows into an elegant web app that captures the right data at the right time.

Idea — 2022: a clinician-designed prototype
Build — 2023: MVP and early clinic pilots
Scale — 2024: wider deployments and refinements
